NITHILA & SEYON

Family Fun Games 🏠

🕵️ Who Threw It?

How to play
  1. Nithila (8) and Seyon (3) are having a throwing war 💥
  2. The moment a grown-up walks in, they freeze and watch TV like little angels 😇
  3. Tap the 🔍 spots to find clues. You only get a few magnifiers, and some clues are fake!
  4. Point at the kid who threw the LAST one. Fewer magnifiers used = more points ⭐
🎧 Tip: with headphones, the final THUD in the dark comes from the victim's side!

🏁 Race Day! Who finishes first?

How to play
  1. The screen splits in two: 👧 Nithila on the left, 👦 Seyon on the right (top and bottom on an upright phone).
  2. Tap your own big button as fast as you can to finish each job. Laptop keys: A S D for Nithila, J K L for Seyon.
  3. Watch out for surprises! Some need fast tapping, some need waiting, and some need you to pick the right thing.
  4. First to finish the last job wins! 🏆

🏎️ TURBO RACERS

Nithila vs Seyon · split-screen street racing
How to play
  1. Pick your ride: sports car, superbike, Chennai auto rickshaw or go-kart.
  2. Your ride speeds up by itself, so just steer! 👧 Nithila: A / D steer, W nitro 🔥, S horn 📯. 👦 Seyon: ← / → steer, ↑ nitro, ↓ horn. On a phone or tablet, use the ◀ ▶ 🔥 📯 buttons.
  3. Grab 🪙 coins and ⚡ nitro cans. Stay on the road and don't bonk into buses, autos or cows!
  4. 📯 Honk to make traffic move aside (and to tease your opponent 😝). First across the finish line after 2 laps wins! 🏁

🧟 MONSTER CHASE 🐋

Nithila vs Seyon · first to the finish wins!
How to play
  1. A silly monster is chasing each of you! Amma & Appa are waiting at the finish line, and monsters are scared of Amma 😄
  2. Tap RUN again and again to go fast. Tap JUMP to hop over rocks, logs and jellyfish. 👧 Nithila: A or D to run, W to jump. 👦 Seyon: ← or → to run, ↑ to jump.
  3. Grab ⭐ stars for super speed. If the monster catches you, tap RUN super fast to wriggle free! 💪
  4. First one across the finish line wins. Get caught with no wriggles left and you're out! 🏁
